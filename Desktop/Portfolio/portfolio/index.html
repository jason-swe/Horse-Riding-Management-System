I'm a final-year student at VNU University of Education, majoring in Educational Technology Management (GPA 3.5/4.0). My mission: to bridge the digital divide in education through thoughtful, scalable design.

Over the past three years, I've worn many hats — e-learning architect, teacher trainer, research lead, community builder. Each role has reinforced one belief: technology should never replace pedagogy, only amplify it.

From training 200+ teachers across rural Vietnamese schools, to designing AI-integrated learning materials for national curriculum partners, to founding the first EdTech student club at my university — I build at the intersection of research, design, and impact.

Currently building nextSTEM Vietnam, an AI-enabled platform helping students discover STEM career pathways through personalized learning. Open to international collaborations, internships, and research partnerships.
